# Learn page review checklist (brief §20.4)

Stub — the content pipeline is Phase 9. A learn page builds only with `review_status: approved`.

Before setting a page to `approved`, confirm:

- [ ] No treat / cure / prevent language anywhere.
- [ ] Every grade has citations and matches the rubric output.
- [ ] Anecdotes are labelled as anecdotes and contain no quotes, usernames, product or brand names.
- [ ] The forms table matches `config/compounds.yml`.
- [ ] UK spelling.
- [ ] The fixed line is present: "If you take medication, are pregnant, or have a health condition, check with a pharmacist or GP first."
